# Summary of the Petition to the Strategy Development Department of the Ministry of National Education

**Date:** 13/10/2025
**Recipient:** Ministry of National Education – Strategy Development Department (Turkey)
**Subject:** Request for Data and Technical Information for the *Student Exam Generator and Analyzer System (S.E.S.)* Project

---

## Purpose of the Project

The **S.E.S. Project** aims to improve how schools prepare and analyze exams by providing:

* More efficient exam creation tools
* Comprehensive result analysis
* Digital support for educational management processes

The project is **open-source**, **transparent**, and open to collaboration and feedback from the community.

---

## Requests Made

1. **Technical Documentation and Data Formats**

   * Existing data structures used in current school management systems
   * API specifications
   * Data dictionaries
   * Sample datasets

2. **Integration Details**

   * Technical connection methods
   * Authentication or test environment information for integration and testing

3. **Pilot Study Permission**

   * Approval to conduct limited observations and a small-scale pilot in two schools located in Edirne city center
   * To evaluate the project under real conditions

---

## Data Usage and Compliance

* All data will be **anonymized** and used only for **aggregated analysis**
* Analytical / machine-based models will remain limited to this scope
* Personal data will be protected in accordance with **KVKK (Turkish Personal Data Protection Law)** and **Ministry of National Education regulations**
* No data will be shared with third parties
